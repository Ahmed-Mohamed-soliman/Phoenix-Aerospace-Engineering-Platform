# Drag Force

## Definition

Drag is the aerodynamic force that opposes the aircraft's motion through the air.

## Equation

D = 0.5 × ρ × V² × S × CD

## Variables

| Symbol | Description | Unit |
|---------|-------------|------|
| D | Drag Force | Newton (N) |
| ρ | Air Density | kg/m³ |
| V | Velocity | m/s |
| S | Wing Area | m² |
| CD | Drag Coefficient | Dimensionless |

## Assumptions

- Steady Flight
- Standard Atmosphere
- Constant Drag Coefficient
