# Lift Force

## Definition

Lift is the aerodynamic force acting perpendicular to the relative airflow generated by an aircraft wing.

## Lift Equation

L = 0.5 × ρ × V² × S × CL

## Variables

| Symbol | Description | Unit |
|---------|-------------|------|
| L | Lift Force | Newton (N) |
| ρ | Air Density | kg/m³ |
| V | Velocity | m/s |
| S | Wing Area | m² |
| CL | Lift Coefficient | Dimensionless |

## Assumptions

- Steady Flight
- Standard Atmosphere
- Incompressible Flow
- Constant Lift Coefficient
